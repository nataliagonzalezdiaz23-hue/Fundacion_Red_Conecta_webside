About us

Non-profit organization
Bogotá, Colombia

Fundación Red Conecta provides comprehensive, continuous support to talented students with limited economic opportunities as they build their educational and professional path.

We identify candidates in public secondary schools, assess their profile and their circumstances, secure with higher education institutions the benefits that make enrollment possible, and sustain academic and psychosocial support throughout their studies.

Scope of our work

Corporate purpose

To promote access, retention and the educational and professional development of talented students with limited economic opportunities, through career guidance, academic and psychosocial support, mentoring, monitoring and the connection of opportunities, as well as through the development and strengthening of alliances with schools, universities and public and private organizations, both national and international.

Nature of our work

The Foundation does not provide formal, non-formal or higher education, and does not grant degrees, academic certificates or educational credentials. Its work is limited to informal education: guidance, support, preparation, advice, mentoring and complementary assistance to processes carried out by legally authorized institutions.

Outside our scope

  • We do not grant degrees or academic certifications.
  • We do not guarantee the award of scholarships.
  • We do not transfer funds directly to students.
  • We do not replace the academic, administrative or student welfare functions of higher education institutions.

Additional information

Personal data processing

We collect and process personal data in accordance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013. Any student’s participation requires the informed consent of their guardian, and individual information is shared with partner institutions only with prior authorization and under the principle of data minimization.

To access, update or delete your data, or to request a copy of our data processing policy, write to us at fundacionredconecta@gmail.com.
